What's New in v1.1

August 31, 2019

Based on feedback from users, we are excited to announce two new features for SeeSpotBid!

New Pricing Structure

In order to simplify pricing options, Search Alerts are now FREE to create and no longer expire. Search frequency choices are also simplified, with an option to pay for faster searches if desired. If you previously purchased Search Alerts in the old version, we've transferred your purchases to a (greater than) equivalent Fast Search subscription. For example, if you paid $5 for a 1yr Search Alert, we've applied a 3-year Fast Search subscription to your profile (a $15 value).

International eBay Sites

In addition to the US site, you can now search all international eBay sites: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Italy, Malaysia, eBay Motors, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, Singapore, Spain, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. You can change your default site in Settings.


We hope you enjoy using the new app! If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
