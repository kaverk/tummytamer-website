# Beta Testing
Tummy Tamer has entered the public beta testing phase. Find the information you need below.

## What is Tummy Tamer?
Tummy Tamer is a free food diary app meant to help you uncover food sensitivities and triggers. There are lots of digestive issues out there, and our app makes it easy to uncover how certain foods, drinks, and other factors affect your stomach and gut. Check out our [home page](https://tummytamer.app) for a list of features.

## How to Join the Beta 
You can download Tummy Tamer at the following links. Apple distributes all beta apps through TestFlight, so the iOS link will walk you through how to download that.
* Download for [Android](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kaverk.tummytamer)
* Download for [iPhone](https://testflight.apple.com/join/pl8Oba0x)

## What Should I Test?
Anything and everything: onboarding experience, first impressions, ease-of-use, history view, search feature, colors/icons, reminder notifications, etc. **The one feature missing from the app is report generation with all your data-- we're still working on that.**

## Sharing Feedback
You can [email us](mailto:beta@kaverk.com) or use the "Contact" button in the Settings screen. 

## Final Thoughts
1. The app only becomes truly useful after a few weeks of entering data so trends can be seen, so give it a chance. For the Beta, some test data has been randomly generated for the previous month so that you can explore the History views. 
2. We don't have a way of knowing who downloads the Beta because users are kept anonymous. We can't email you asking for feedback, so please be proactive in sharing! 
3. This is an early beta, so the polish of the look and feel will definitely improve before final release. We'd still love your thoughts on those details, though.

**Thanks for your help! Our hope is that your feedback and this app will help thousands of people find relief from digestive issues.**
