Why Tummy Tamer?

You've heard the buzz words: Gluten free. Dairy free. IBS. Elimination diets. FODMAP. Inflammation. Keto. Fasting.

Which one is right for you?

Your body is complicated. Gut health affects mood, energy, pain levels, and many other things that researchers are still finding out. Millions of us suffer from some sort of stomach issue. Tummy Tamer is here to help you understand your own body better. Good record-keeping is crucial to finding digestive relief, and Tummy Tamer makes it easy to track the effects of different factors in your diet and life.

Tummy Tamer is for:

  • Those with stomach issues
    Track what you eat, what you drink, and other metrics like stress, monthly cycles, and calendar events to see the effect on your symptoms.
  • Mothers with babies
    Keep a record of your baby's symptoms to see how their food (and yours, when breast-feeding) affects their mood, fussiness, stool, etc.
  • Athletes
    Find correlations between your eating habits and your workout performance (energy levels, strength, attitude, etc.).

Our Story

I started investigating my own digestive issues two years ago. When I talked to my doctor, I was referred to a gastroenterologist, and things got complicated quickly with follow-up visits and medications. I decided to take a step back and start with a simple Food Diary (on paper) to track my food intake and symptoms. I learned a lot about my body and what foods it didn't like, but the record-keeping process was arduous and it was almost impossible to see trends. That's when I started to create Tummy Tamer, with the goal of helping others in the same boat as me find relief from digestive issues.

Chris K

Lead Developer
